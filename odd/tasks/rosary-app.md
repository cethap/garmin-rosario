# Rosary app

## Objective

A fast, retro-styled Connect IQ widget for the Garmin Instinct 2 that guides the full Rosary bead by bead, picks the mysteries of the day automatically, and is reachable in two presses from the watch face.

## Problem

The user's current rosary app is slow and does not sync the mysteries with the current day. A rosary mode inside the watch face is impossible: watch faces do not accept user input and the LIGHT button belongs to the system (verified in the Connect IQ docs).

## Why

The user prays the Rosary daily with the watch and wants quick, low-friction navigation with tactile feedback.

## Scope

Authorized by the user ("dale, arranquemos con la app del rosario en su propio repo"; structure chosen: "Completo con Salve").

- Widget app with a glance in the Instinct 2 glance loop (DOWN from the watch face, then START).
- Sequence (72 beads): Credo, Padre Nuestro, 3 Avemarías, Gloria; then 5 mysteries × (Padre Nuestro, 10 Avemarías, Gloria, Oh Jesús mío); then Salve.
- Mysteries by weekday: Monday/Saturday Gozosos, Tuesday/Friday Dolorosos, Wednesday/Sunday Gloriosos, Thursday Luminosos.
- Buttons: START or DOWN next bead, UP previous bead, BACK exit (progress saved for the day). Hold UP (MENU) starts the day over after a YES/NO confirmation (user request after the on-device check: "no hay opción de resetear").
- Read the current mystery (user request after using the widget on the watch: "me gustaría que pudiera tener una opción como donde está el reinicio pero para leer más sobre el misterio actual 1, 2, 3, 4 y 5, como una cita bíblica referente a ese misterio"): hold UP opens a menu with "Leer el misterio" and "Reiniciar"; the reading view shows the mystery title, its name and a short Bible quote with its reference. Refined by the user: "que pueda bajar con los botones de up y down cuando esté viendo el texto y regresar con back, sería genial que no se solapen con el anillo que me pasa mucho cuando leo mensajes que me llegan desde el celular": UP/DOWN scroll, BACK returns, and no text ever goes under the subscreen ring.
- Vibration: short pulse on each bead. When the 10 Avemarías of a decade end and the Gloria starts, a distinct multi-pulse pattern (3 pulses), so the user feels it without looking (user request). After the on-device check the user asked for it stronger and easier to tell apart ("me gustaría más vibración cada gloria que se pueda diferenciar mucho mejor"). The opening Gloria, after the 3 Avemarías, vibrates the same way (user report: "el primer gloria que es luego de los tres avemarías no tiene zumbido"). A long vibration at the end of the Rosary.
- Small time on screen; the 10 Avemarías of the current decade as a 10-segment ring in the subscreen.
- Menu, after using it on the watch (R15): the menu and the Bible quote work well, but "creo que los textos del menú pueden ser más grandes". The system `Menu2` draws its items in its own small font, which an app cannot change, so the app draws its own menu: "Leer misterio" and "Reiniciar" in the large font, the selected one inverted (white bar, black text), under the current mystery's title.
- Glance, after using it on the watch (R13): keep the system's rosary icon in the subscreen; the set name and the status are too far apart, so they should read as one block; "Inicio" during the opening sounds like "not started", so it reads "En progreso"; "Completado" stays. The user confirmed the earlier clipping showed only in the simulator: on the watch the glance looks right.

Out of scope for the MVP: liturgical-season variants (Advent or Lent Sundays), full prayer texts, settings, other devices.

## Constraints

- Target: Instinct 2 (176x176 monochrome MIP, subscreen at x=113 y=0 62x62), Connect IQ 3.4.2. Memory limits: widget 64KB, glance 32KB.
- Spanish content (prayer and mystery names). Code, comments and docs in English.
- The pixel font from the watch face has no accented characters or ñ. Spanish text uses the system fonts, which include them.
- Conventional commits, no AI attribution. Push, GitHub repo creation and merges are the user's decisions.

## Acceptance criteria

- `monkeyc -d instinct2` builds with 0 warnings. `monkeydo -t` passes every test.
- The sequence has 72 steps in the chosen order. The weekday mapping matches the scope.
- Next and prev never go out of range. Progress survives exiting and reopening the same day, and resets on a new day.
- The glance shows the day's mysteries right above the progress as one block (pitch 19), and the opening reads "En progreso".
- Every Gloria reached forward (after the 3 opening Avemarías and after the 10 of each decade) vibrates as 3 or more full-strength pulses lasting at least 1.2s, distinct from the bead and end pulses, within one `Attention.vibrate` call.
- Hold UP opens the app's menu: the current mystery's title (and its number in the subscreen) over "Leer misterio" and "Reiniciar" in the large font, "Leer misterio" selected first, the selected item a white bar with black text; every text and bar keeps the 2px margin inside the visible area at both selections. UP/DOWN move the selection and stop at either end; START closes the menu and opens the item over the rosary; BACK returns to the same bead; hold UP does nothing there. "Reiniciar" asks "¿Reiniciar el rosario?": YES clears today's progress ("Sin empezar") and returns to the Credo; NO changes nothing.
- "Leer misterio" shows the current mystery (opening: the 1st; decade n: the n-th; Salve and done: the 5th) with a verbatim Bible quote and its reference. UP/DOWN scroll and stop at both ends, UP goes back through the same pages DOWN showed, BACK returns to the same bead, hold UP does nothing there; no vibration, no change to the progress. No text goes under the subscreen ring at any scroll position, and every quote reads in 3 screens at most. An open reading view holds less than 3072 bytes.
